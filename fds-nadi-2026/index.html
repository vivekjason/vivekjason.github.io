Technical methods, sources and detailed downloads

The three original Excel workbooks are unchanged. Geographic calculations use R sf with s2 on WGS84 longitude/latitude. The closest listed NADI is selected across all 287 centres with usable coordinates in both states, regardless of operational status. The full distance matrix is searched, including centres outside the buffer. Distances are geodesic straight-line distances in km, not road, river or flight-route lengths.

Coverage uses a 5 km radius. Colour thresholds are green <5 km, yellow 5–10 km inclusive, and red >10 km, applied before rounding. The original 1/3 km bands are also retained in the distance CSV. Repeated coordinates are retained as separate service records in CSVs and represented once on maps.

The FDS named “nearest KK” field is used for the requested clinic linkage, as confirmed by the user. It is not an independently verified clinic-in-charge relationship. Six documented name aliases are listed in the crosswalk audit. PKD/PKB offices are not silently substituted with clinics. Geographically nearest KK results are provided in separate columns for every FDS.

The main nearest-centre search includes two NADI records under relocation and one temporarily closed for maintenance. Only the separate open-only comparison excludes them and uses 284 centres. The NADI open at a temporary location uses the coordinates explicitly included in its operation-status note. One NADI, PPR Taman Sri Semarak in Tawau, has coordinates in Peninsular Malaysia and is excluded pending correction. Eleven health-register records, including eight KK, have zero coordinates or coordinates in Peninsular Malaysia. The KK distance layer contains 338 clinics. NADI coordinate exceptions and health-facility coordinate exceptions retain the originals. KK “Status” describes its source system context and is not used to infer that a clinic is physically closed.

DOSM district GeoJSON supplies boundaries; state outlines are dissolved from districts. Geofabrik’s OpenStreetMap extract contains data through 2026-09-08T20:21:01Z. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL; attribution and licence. No mapped feature does not mean no feature exists.

Elevation uses Mapzen Terrain Tiles on AWS, zoom 11 GeoTIFFs, resampled bilinearly to a 40 m grid in the local UTM zone (49N or 50N). Slope uses terra’s eight-neighbour method in degrees. Relief is maximum minus minimum elevation. These are regional terrain indicators, not building-level engineering, flood modelling or radio-propagation results. SRTM and GMTED2010 terrain data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey; full terrain attribution.

Candidate facilities are searched within 10 km and ranked only by distance. Sources are OSM facility tags, the supplied health-facility register and explicit school names in FDS. Features explicitly tagged as religious, commercial or privately owned/operated are excluded from the public/civic shortlist and recorded in the exclusion audit. A point on each OSM footprint represents a facility; it may not be an entrance. Name-matched duplicates within 100 m are linked in the source audit. Unnamed buildings within a school campus may remain as separate candidate features; a field inventory must establish distinct premises. Public ownership of the remaining candidates is not assumed. Property availability, room capacity, power, connectivity, structural condition, permissions and access need field verification.

Mapped roads exclude tracks and paths, which are reported separately. Road/river proximity uses the feature geometry inside the 10 km search area. Candidate line–waterway intersections count intersected OSM reaches along a straight line; they are not verified river crossings or travel routes. Access tags and mapped surface tags are retained where available. Terrain across the international boundary may appear on the map, but the OSM extract does not establish complete coverage outside Malaysia.
